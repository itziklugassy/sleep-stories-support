6.Sensitive information and category selection

The stories are organized in 6 categories: Tourette syndrome, attention and focus (ADHD), worries and fears, trouble falling asleep, sensory sensitivity, and autism spectrum. We are aware that some category names refer to health or developmental conditions. Therefore:

  • Choosing a category is not a collection of health information about the Child. It is a content choice, like choosing a shelf in a library. It does not indicate that the Child has any diagnosis, and we draw no such inference from it.
  • The choice is not linked to your identity by us, and we keep no record of it. When you open a category, the App sends Firebase a query by category name only, with no user identifier, without the RevenueCat Anonymous ID and without authentication. The hosting provider (Google) receives, as with any network request, technical data such as an IP address.
  • The choice is not sent to RevenueCat, is not used for advertising or profiling, and is not sold or shared.
  • The list of opened stories (used to count the free stories) is stored on the device only.

Because we do not process health information about an identified or identifiable person, we do not process "specially sensitive information" under Israeli law, "health data" under Article 9 of the GDPR, or "sensitive personal information" under the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not ask for consent to do so. If this changes, we will update this Policy and act in accordance with the law, including obtaining explicit consent where required.

11.Data security

The best way to protect information is not to collect it, so the App is built on data minimization. In addition:

  • All communication between the App and the service providers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Local data is kept in the App's private storage on the device and protected by iOS security mechanisms (sandbox).
  • Access to the Apple, RevenueCat, Firebase, Expo, Gmail and GitHub dashboards is limited to the Operator and protected by two-factor authentication.
  • The Firestore database is configured as read-only for the App: story content can be read from it, not written to it.
  • We take reasonable measures in line with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Regulations (Data Security), 5777-2017, to the extent they apply, and at a security level appropriate to the nature of the information.

No security method is completely immune, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. However, because of the very limited scope of the information, the risk to your privacy is low.
